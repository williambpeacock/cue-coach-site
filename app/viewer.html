  • Angles come from the gyro and are measured against where the cue pointed at contact. "Tip" numbers are those angles at the grip-to-tip distance set in the settings.
  • The grip is drawn sliding straight along the line. Only the cue's turning is measured, not the whole cue sliding sideways or up and down.
  • For 30 ms after contact the hit shakes the sensor, so the direction is held still there (the grey band on the chart).
  • Left and right are as the shooter sees them, standing behind the cue (confirmed with deliberate steers, 2026-11-07). In the top view the shooter's left is up the screen.
