Cue Coach

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

In short:

  • Cue Coach records how your cue moves so we can show you, and your coach if you have one, what your stroke is doing.
  • We keep your name, email, your shots and your drill results. That's what the service is made of.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time. We then remove your name and everything that identifies you; we may keep your shots without it, to improve the measurements.
  • Cue Coach is for people 13 and older.

Who we are

Cue Coach is made by Cue Coach LLC ("Cue Coach", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Cue Coach apps for iPhone and Android, the sensor that goes on your cue, our website at cue-coach.com, the My Cue Coach web page (cue-coach.com/app), and the emails we send. Questions go to team@cue-coach.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your name, email address and player code (a short code such as "AB01" that labels your shots).
  • Whether you're a player or a coach, and which hand you play with.
  • Your password is handled by our login provider and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We can't see it.

Your shots

  • Sensor recordings: how the cue moved and turned during each shot, many hundreds of times a second, and the numbers we work out from them (speed, straightness, timing and so on).
  • When each shot was recorded, and your time zone. We do not collect your location.
  • Your cue (make, weight, tip), the sensor's serial number, name and firmware, and the calibration you do with the sensor.
  • Your phone's make and model, its operating system version and the app's version, which help us find and fix problems.

Drills and coaching

  • Drill results: which shots you made or missed (and which side), where the cue ball ended up, and your scores.
  • If you have a coach: invitations, the drills they assign, your tests and their results, the reports made from them, and the comments you and your coach send each other. A coach can also keep private notes about a student.

Video and voice

  • Video shot films a shot with your phone's camera. The clip stays on your phone; it goes somewhere only if you share it. (Some early test phones also copy recordings and clips to Cue Coach's Google Drive. That stops as those phones move to the current app.)
  • Voice marks let you say "yes", "no", "left" or "right" to mark a drill shot. Your phone's own speech recognition turns the words into a mark: Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android. Depending on your phone's settings, the sound may be sent to Apple or Google to be recognized. Cue Coach keeps only the mark, never the sound or the words.

When you sign up or visit our booth

  • The sign-up form on cue-coach.com and our trade show page ask for your name, email, whether you're a player or a coach, your cue, and whether you'd like to test Cue Coach or hear from us. At the booth we also ask you to confirm you're 13 or older and that you agree to this policy.

When you buy

  • Purchases go through Square. Square handles your card; we never see or store card numbers. We receive what we need to send your order: your name, email and shipping address.

On our website

  • The My Cue Coach web page saves a few things in your browser so you stay signed in and it remembers your display choices (for example which drill you were looking at).
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use analytics services.

How we use it

  • To run Cue Coach: measure your shots, show you the results on your phone and on the web page, keep your drills and history, and let you and your coach work together.
  • To send emails you need: sign-in and password links, coaching invitations, test results and reminders, and order updates. News and offers only if you said yes, and you can stop them any time.
  • To make the measurements better: we study recordings to check and improve how Cue Coach measures a stroke. This is done by Cue Coach staff, inside Cue Coach. When we use recordings beyond your own account, for example to test a new measurement across many players, we look at the movement and the numbers, not at who you are.
  • To help you and keep Cue Coach working: answer your questions, find faults, and keep accounts secure.

Who can see your information

  • You.
  • Your coach, while you're linked to them: your shots, drills, tests, reports and comments. When the link ends, they stop seeing anything new from you.
  • Cue Coach staff, to support you and improve the measurements. Only the people who need it can see it.
  • Borrowed sensors: if you use someone else's sensor, your shots go to your account. The sensor's owner doesn't see them.
  • Shared demo accounts: at events we sometimes let people try Cue Coach on a shared account. Shots recorded on it can be seen by others using it, and we clear it regularly. Use your own account for anything you want to keep private.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account and shots for as long as your account is open, so your history and progress are there when you come back.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, within 30 days we delete your account, your name and email, your coaching comments, notes and reports, and anything else that identifies you. Backup copies are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • We may keep your shots without your name. The sensor recordings, the numbers worked out from them and their drill results are kept with nothing that identifies you: no name, email, player code or account. We use them only to check and improve how Cue Coach measures a stroke, and we don't try to work out whose they were. If you'd rather your shots were deleted too, say so when you ask and we'll delete them as well.
  • Sign-up and booth details are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until we no longer need them to follow up.
  • We may keep records we're required to keep by law, such as for sales and taxes.

Your choices

  • See or download your information: your shots are on the My Cue Coach web page, or ask us for a copy.
  • Correct it: change your name, hand and cue in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete your account: use Delete account in the app when it's available, or email team@cue-coach.com from the email address on your account. We remove everything that identifies you and keep your shots without your name (see "How long we keep it"), or delete your shots too if you ask.
  • Stop news emails: use the link in the email, or tell us.
  • Phone permissions: you can turn off the camera, microphone or speech recognition for Cue Coach in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Some places, including California and the European Union, give you specific privacy rights. We offer the choices above to everyone, wherever you live. We answer within 30 days and never treat you differently for asking.

Children

Cue Coach is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email team@cue-coach.com and we'll delete all of it, shots included.

Keeping it safe

Everything between the apps, the website and our servers travels encrypted. Access rules in our database mean each person sees only what they're allowed to: you see your own shots, a coach sees only their linked students. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example we start collecting something new, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
