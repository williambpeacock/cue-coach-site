How coaching works

Invite. Assign. Test. Review.

You and your student each use your own phone and My Cue Coach, the Cue Coach web page. Emails tell each of you when it's your turn.

  1. 1

    Invite

    Invite a student by email from My Cue Coach. Once they accept, you see their shots.

  2. 2

    Assign

    Pick a drill and level from the Cue Coach drill list and how many sets of ten make a test.

  3. 3

    Test

    Your student shoots the test on their phone. You get an email when it's in.

  4. 4

    Review

    Open the report, set the next target, move them up a level or on to a new drill. They practise for a week, then test again.

After every test

The Cue Coach Report.

Each test comes back to you as one report, ready when you open it.

  • Score and goalThe score and each set's score, whether the goal and the stroke target were met, and a suggested next target.
  • Trouble areasWhere the stroke broke down, set by set.
  • Made and missedHow the stroke differed on the shots that went in and the ones that didn't.
  • Best and worstThe best and worst shots by the number you're working on, and every shot one by one.

The report suggests. You decide.

On My Cue Coach

Everything about each student, in one place.

  • Coaches CornerWhere each student is at a glance: a test waiting for review, practising, when the next test is due.
  • Every shotAll of a student's shots and their numbers, practice and tests.
  • ScoreboardEach student's drill scores: last score, personal best, recent average and trend.
  • Shot pathReplay a shot's cue path: where the cue pointed, how it twisted, how far it travelled.
  • Comments and notesComment on a test for your student, and keep private notes they never see.
  • Fargo ratingKeep each student's Fargo rating beside their numbers.

Cue Coach doesn't replace a coach.

It gives you and your student the same clear picture of the stroke. You see what's really happening, they see it too, and you both see it improve.
