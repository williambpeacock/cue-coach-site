How it works

See exactly what your stroke is doing.

Cue Coach is a small sensor that screws into the butt of your cue, just like an extension, and records every stroke. The app shows you what your stroke really did.

  1. 1

    Screw it on

    It threads into the butt of your own cue, just like an extension. No changes to the cue.

  2. 2

    Shoot like normal

    Cue Coach records the motion of your stroke and sends it to your phone.

  3. 3

    See what to fix

    The app breaks the stroke down and points out the flaws, like twist or bad acceleration.

Who it's for

For players. For coaches.

Cue Coach doesn't replace a coach. It gives the player and the coach the same clear picture of the stroke.

  • Players

    See your own flaws, shot by shot, and know exactly what to work on in practice.

  • Coaches

    Show students what their stroke is really doing, back up what you see with data, and track their improvement.

Every phase, scored

Your whole stroke, start to finish.

Cue Coach splits each stroke into its phases so you can see exactly where it goes wrong.

  • Address
  • Backstroke
  • Pause
  • Forward stroke
  • Impact
  • Follow-through
