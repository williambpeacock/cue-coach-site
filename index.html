How it works

See exactly what your stroke is doing.

Cue Coach™ is a small sensor that fits in place of your cue's bumper and records every stroke. The app shows you what your stroke really did.

  1. 1

    Fit it

    It fits in place of your cue's bumper. No changes to the cue.

  2. 2

    Shoot like normal

    Cue Coach records the motion of your stroke and sends it to your phone.

  3. 3

    See what to fix

    The app breaks the stroke down and points out the flaws, like twist or bad acceleration.

The app

What you see on every shot.

Take a shot and the numbers are on your phone straight away.

  • Impact speedHow fast the cue meets the ball.
  • Stroke lengthBackstroke, forward stroke and follow-through.
  • TempoBack and forward timing, and follow-through time.
  • Cue twistHow much the cue turns in your hand.
  • Straightness and timingHow straight the cue travels and how it builds speed.
  • Settled and stillSettled at address, still at the finish.

Pick which numbers show and in what order. Left- and right-handed players are both supported.

Every phase

Your whole stroke, start to finish.

Cue Coach splits each stroke into its phases so you can see exactly where it goes wrong.

  • Address
  • Backstroke
  • Forward stroke
  • Impact
  • Follow-through

Ways to practise

Practise the way you play.

  • Single shotTake a shot and see its numbers straight away.
  • Free PlayRun a rack or play a game, then see your high, low and average for each number.
  • DrillsSet up the balls as shown and mark each shot made or missed by tapping or by voice, so you never put the cue down.
  • Video shotFilm a shot with your phone and watch it back with its numbers.
  • HistoryEvery shot you've taken, ready to open again.

Who it's for

For players. For coaches.

Cue Coach doesn't replace a coach. It gives the player and the coach the same clear picture of the stroke.

Players

See your own flaws, shot by shot, and know exactly what to work on in practice.

Coaches

Show students what their stroke is really doing, back up what you see with data, and track their improvement.

  • See every student's shots on My Cue Coach
  • Give drills and tests, and get a report after each test
